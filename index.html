01 — About

I am an AI engineer with a Master's in Language Technology from the University of Gothenburg and six years of experience across data analysis, software engineering and applied AI. My work focuses on natural language processing, large language models and multimodal human–AI interaction.

Most recently I designed a hybrid NLP and machine translation pipeline with Volvo Trucks, combining terminology-aware rule-based methods with LLM refinement. For my Master's thesis I built a real-time multimodal conversational system integrating LLMs, vision-language models and the Furhat social robot, and ran user studies on human–AI experience design.

My path into AI came through civil engineering, data analysis and technical sales. That background shapes how I work: I care about rigour, but also about systems that hold up with real users and real constraints.

Combining subjects that seem opposite.

Art and mathematics. Meditation and robotics. Engineering and storytelling. These are not opposites to me — they are different lenses on the same problem.

My thesis is the clearest example: a social robot, powered by large language models and vision-language models, guiding a human through a meditation experience. One is quiet and inward; the other is engineered and outward.

Building it forced me to ask what human–AI interaction really means when the AI is not answering questions but shaping an experience. The technical work was only half of it — the other half was understanding how people actually respond.
