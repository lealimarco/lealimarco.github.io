01 — About

Combining subjects that seem opposite.

Art and mathematics. Humans and robots. If AI is the ultimate optimisation, so the language is the ultimate art.

My thesis is the clearest example: a social robot, powered by large language models and vision-language models, guiding participants through a meditation experience settled in Stockholm.

Most recently I designed a hybrid NLP and machine translation pipeline with Volvo Trucks, combining terminology-aware rule-based methods with LLM refinement. For my Master's thesis I built a real-time multimodal conversational system integrating LLMs, vision-language models and the Furhat social robot, and ran user studies on human–AI experience design.
